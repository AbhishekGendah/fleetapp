# Privacy policy template for Operators (DRAFT)

**Status:** first draft, written by AGS from the OAIC's guidance on APP
privacy policies (Australian Privacy Principle 1). Not legal advice. To be
reviewed by a lawyer once before the first real Operator uses it (see
docs/launch-checklist.md). Last updated 31 Oct 2026.

## Notes for the Operator (delete this section before publishing)

- This is **your** privacy policy, about the people **you** deal with:
  your renters, their emergency contacts, and people who enquire. You are
  responsible for it being true for your business. AGS supplies it as a
  starting point only.
- Replace everything in **[square brackets]**. Delete any section marked
  *optional* that you do not use (for example, if your cars have no GPS
  trackers, delete that section).
- If you collect anything not listed here (for example, a copy of a
  passport, or bank statements), add it. If you stop collecting something,
  take it out.
- Publish it somewhere renters can read it for free: your website, and a
  copy you can email or hand over on request (APP 1.5).
- Review it once a year, and whenever you change how you work.
- **Never collect what you do not need.** In particular, do not record
  criminal history, health or similar details about a renter: that is
  "sensitive information" under the Privacy Act and needs consent and
  extra care.
- Small businesses (under $3 million turnover) are often exempt from the
  Privacy Act, but not always. Even if you are exempt, following this
  policy is good practice and what renters expect. If unsure, ask a
  lawyer.

# [Business trading name] privacy policy

[Legal name of business], ABN [ABN] ("we", "us") rents vehicles to
people ("you"). This policy explains what personal information we collect
about you, why, who we share it with, and how you can see it, correct it
or complain.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

## 1. What we collect

When you rent from us, or ask to, we collect:

- **Who you are and how to reach you:** your full name, any preferred
  name, phone number, email address, date of birth, home address and
  postal address.
- **Your driver licence:** where it was issued, the licence number, class,
  type (full or provisional) and expiry date, and photos or scans of the
  front and back.
- **An emergency contact:** their name, phone number and relationship to
  you. Please let them know you have given us their details.
- **Your rentals:** which vehicle you had and when, rental agreements you
  signed, handover and return reports and photos (which may show you or
  your belongings), odometer readings, damage, and any fines, tolls or
  infringement notices received while you had the vehicle.
- **Payments:** what you have paid and owe. Your bank account or card
  details are collected and held by our payment provider (Stripe), not by
  us. [Delete this item until you take payments through the system.]
- **Notes we make** about our dealings with you, and whether we have
  decided not to rent to you again and why. We write facts only. You can
  ask to see these (section 6).
- **Messages** between you and us (emails, texts, calls notes).
- **Technical information** when you use a link we send you: a private
  cookie that remembers your phone for that link, and the basic details
  every website receives (such as your device's internet address), kept
  in our service providers' server logs.
- *Optional:* **Vehicle location and driving data** from GPS trackers
  fitted to our vehicles: [what is recorded, e.g. location, speed, ignition
  on/off], [when, e.g. at all times / only if the vehicle is reported
  stolen or overdue]. [Check the surveillance devices law in your state
  before using trackers.]
- *Optional:* **[Anything else you collect, e.g. rideshare driver
  accreditation, a reference.]**

Most of this comes from you: when you fill in our details form (sent by
link to your phone), sign an agreement, or talk to us. Some comes from
others: government agencies and toll operators (about fines and tolls),
[rideshare platforms, if you tell us to contact them], and our payment
provider.

If you do not give us these details, we may not be able to rent to you.

## 2. Why we collect it

We use your information to:

- check who you are and that you are licensed to drive the vehicle;
- prepare, sign and manage your rental agreement;
- hand over and take back the vehicle, and record its condition;
- take payments, and recover money you owe us;
- deal with fines, tolls and infringements, including telling the issuing
  authority who was driving (nominating you) when the law allows;
- deal with damage, accidents, insurance claims and theft;
- contact you, or your emergency contact if we cannot reach you or
  something urgent happens with the vehicle;
- keep the records the law requires (for example, tax records); and
- [any other purpose, e.g. checking rideshare eligibility].

We do not sell your information. [We do not send you marketing.] *or*
[We may send you offers about our own vehicles. Every marketing message
lets you unsubscribe, and we will stop when you ask.]

## 3. Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed for the purposes above, with:

- **Government agencies and toll operators**, such as police, state road
  authorities and fine enforcement agencies, for fines, tolls and
  infringements, or when the law requires it;
- **Our insurer and repairers**, for damage, accidents and claims;
- **Debt recovery agents or lawyers**, if you owe us money;
- **Our service providers**, who hold or process information for us under
  contract:
  - **PintoFleet** (provided by Altus Global Solutions, Perth WA), the
    system we use to manage renters, vehicles and rentals. It stores your
    information for us in Australia;
  - the providers PintoFleet uses: data storage and hosting in Sydney
    (Amazon Web Services, Neon, Vercel), email delivery (Postmark), address
    search (Google), and payments (Stripe) [delete Stripe until used];
  - [our accountant / bookkeeper];
  - [any others, e.g. an SMS provider, a GPS tracker company].
- **Anyone else you ask us to** share it with.

## 5. How we keep it safe, and for how long

Your information is kept in the PintoFleet system, which:

- keeps each business's information separate from every other business's;
- requires a password and a code from an authenticator app to sign in;
- keeps licence photos and scans in a separate, private store, opens them
  only through short-lived links, and records every time someone views
  one;
- records who created or changed each record;
- does not store bank or card details.

Links we send you to fill in your details are for you only: they expire,
we can cancel them, and the first time they are opened on a phone they
need a code emailed to you. Please do not forward them.

We keep your information while you rent from us and afterwards for as long
as we need it for the purposes above or the law requires (for example, tax
records for 5 years). Photos and scans of your licence are deleted [12]
months after your last rental with us, or after we received them if you
have not rented since, unless a fine or dispute is still open. Details you
send through a link are held for our review and removed within about 30
days of the link ending. Deleted files are removed completely within 30
days. [If you change how long licence photos are kept in Settings, put
your number here.]

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and
the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

## 6. Seeing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us
to correct it, at any time. Contact us (section 8). We will:

- confirm who you are before giving you anything;
- respond within 30 days;
- not charge you for asking. [If a request takes a lot of work, we may
  charge a reasonable fee for providing the information, and will tell you
  first.]

If we refuse a request (only where the law allows), we will tell you why in
writing and how to complain.

You can also correct your contact details and licence yourself through the
details form link we send you; we check the changes before they are saved.

## 7. Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, please contact
us first (section 8). We will acknowledge your complaint within [7] days
and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):
www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.
